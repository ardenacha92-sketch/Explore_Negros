🌿 Why Visit Negros?

Experience the Heart of the Philippines

From majestic volcanoes and cascading waterfalls to the world-renowned MassKara Festival, discover what makes Negros Occidental an unforgettable journey.

🌊
01

Natural Wonders

Hike ancient crater trails at Mt. Kanlaon, dip in volcanic hot springs at Mambukal, and swim in emerald waterfall lagoons hidden deep in the highlands.

Waterfalls Hot Springs Islands
🎭
02

Rich Culture & History

Step through timeless ancestral mansions in Silay Heritage City and experience the vibrant colors, rhythmic beats, and smiles of the world-famous MassKara Festival.

MassKara The Ruins Heritage Houses
🍱
03

Culinary Capital

Savor authentic Bacolod chicken inasal fresh off the charcoal grill, freshly caught coastal seafood, and delectable native piaya, Napoleones, and heirloom sweets.

Chicken Inasal Napoleones Piaya
🤝
04

Warm Negrense Hospitality

Known as the "Land of Smiles", experience genuine Negrense warmth and community spirit that greets every traveler like longtime family.

City of Smiles Welcoming Locals Safe Travels
