About Explore Negros

Explore Negros is a tourism information platform dedicated to showcasing the natural wonders, cultural heritage, and unique experiences that Negros Occidental has to offer. Our mission is to connect travellers with the most breathtaking and authentic destinations across the province.

Whether you're looking for adventure in the mountains, relaxation on pristine island beaches, or a deep dive into the rich cultural heritage of Negros, our platform provides the information and tools you need to plan the perfect visit.

We work in close partnership with local tourism offices, destination managers, and community guides to ensure all information is accurate, up-to-date, and reflective of the true Negrense experience.

How It Works

  1. 1

    Browse Destinations

    Explore our curated list of tourist destinations across Negros Occidental. Filter by activity, location, or interest to find your perfect spot.

  2. 2

    View Destination Details

    Click on any destination to see full details — including activities, facilities, visiting hours, entrance fees, and location information.

  3. 3

    Submit a Visit Request

    Use the Visit Request form to tell us about your planned visit — who is coming, when, and which destination interests you most.

  4. 4

    Get a Confirmation

    Our tourism team will review your request and reach out via email with confirmation details, local tips, and travel recommendations.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is it free to use Explore Negros?
Yes! Explore Negros is completely free to use. Submitting a visit request costs nothing. Entrance fees may apply at individual destinations as listed on each destination's detail page.
How long does it take to get a response after submitting a request?
Our team typically responds within 1–3 business days. During peak travel seasons (April–May and December), response times may be slightly longer. Please ensure your email address is correct when submitting.
Can I visit destinations without submitting a request?
Absolutely. Most destinations on this platform are open to the public and can be visited directly. The visit request form is an optional service for travellers who want personalised assistance and recommendations.
Are the destinations open year-round?
Most destinations are open year-round, but visiting hours and conditions may vary by season. Some destinations may close temporarily during typhoon season or for maintenance. Always check the destination detail page or contact us for the most current information.
How do I report incorrect information about a destination?
Please email us at info@explorenegros.ph with the destination name and the correction you'd like us to make. We strive to keep all information accurate and up-to-date.
Can groups and school trips use this service?
Yes! We welcome individual travellers, families, groups, and school trips. Simply indicate the number of visitors and any special requirements in the message field of the Visit Request form and we'll assist you accordingly.
